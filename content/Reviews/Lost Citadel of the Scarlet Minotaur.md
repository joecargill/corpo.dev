# Prep

In order to focus on the improvised nature of OSR play, allowing for random encounters to determine the dynamic events of the dungeon, I tried to ensure that I did not spend too much time in preparation for running this scenario.

In total I spent about 2 hours reading through about half of the keyed room descriptions, focusing on those near the southern entrance, and any Factions or named NPCs that were likely to show up in the first session.
